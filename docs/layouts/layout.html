ShamsaInk layouts

While you read, the screen holds the page and nothing else. Everything else comes to your hand: a tap brings the bar and the dock, a held margin shows where you are, a held word offers what can be done with it, and the page buttons reach all of it. Every control is a layer over the engine's page, which is never written to. Drawn for the Kobo Libra Colour (1264 × 1680, 7 inches, two page buttons, colour, Bluetooth audio), the first target; nothing in them is Kobo's alone.

Each layer is named by its waveform and when it is refreshed. The outline switch for the layers and tap zones is in the bar above, beside Device and Screen.

Principles

readingThe page alone

No clock, no bar, no ticking: the reader sleeps until a touch or a button.

reachControls come to the hand

The dock at the bottom (one value, to move it if the week of reading says so); the glance beside the finger; in landscape, at the grip.

devicesWhat the device has, only

Light where there is a light, Listen where there is sound, colour on colour screens. Nothing greyed out.

buttonsEverything by buttons too

The page buttons reach every control, for devices and people that need them.

one barThe same bar everywhere

The library's header and the book's bar are one component in one place: the way back or search at the start, the device's time, light and battery at the end.

e-inkRefresh what changed

Each layer its own box and waveform; black and white controls, no shadows, greys or motion.

How it fits together

Two levels at most over the page, and every way out lands on the page: getting back to reading is always one tap.

ruleOut is the page

A tap on the page, Done, a close button or a jump: each closes everything at once and leaves the page alone, one refresh.

ruleTwo levels at most

The page, the bar and dock, one panel. No panel opens another; Contents' tabs are one panel.

ruleThe device's, the book's

The bar holds what belongs to the device (time, headphones, light, battery); the dock what belongs to the book (where you are, its tools).

ruleA way back after a jump

Chapter arrows, a tap on the bar or Contents leave “Back to p. 37” in the place line until two pages are read.

The screens

Library

Where the app starts, and where a book's back arrow returns to. Its header is the bar.

Reading and the dock

The page alone, and the dock one tap away: two ways to show the bar, to compare.

Moving through the book

Chapters, anywhere in the book, and the way back: all from the dock's middle, or from Contents.

Panels from the dock

Each opens from the bar or a tool; every way out goes straight back to the page.

Listening

Started from a held word; a tap pauses and brings the dock; a tap on the page leaves it paused; Play goes on; Listen stops.

On the page

Holds and taps on the page itself.

Variations

Right to left, and turned sideways.

The page buttons

Forward is the lower button whichever way the device is held (the accelerometer says which). A press acts at once; a hold is half a second.
