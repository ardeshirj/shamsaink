How Blitz draws a page, before and after

Experiment 07. One page of Buried Alive (Farsi) and two of Holmes (English), at the e-reader's size, 1072×1448. Numbers are from the reMarkable 2 unless marked Mac.

0The result

Blitz before Blitz now our Go engine KOReader (crengine)

1Before: everything through vello_cpu

Blitz's painter handed every drawing command to vello_cpu, its drawing library. vello_cpu made a 6 MB colour page, and we converted that to grey. Every glyph on every page was turned from its outline into strips of coverage, from scratch.

Blitz's painter→ vello_cpu: every glyph outlined, 127 ms→ colour page rendered, 43 ms→ colour to grey, 23 ms→ grey page

One page of Buried Alive, about 200 ms in all (experiment 06's split). Even a blank page took 57 ms: the colour page and its conversion.

2Tried first: vello_cpu's own glyph cache

vello_cpu has a glyph cache, switched off. Turned on (a one-line patch), it made things worse. Each cached glyph is drawn as a small picture copied pixel by pixel through vello_cpu's general image path, and its cache is one 4096×4096 colour image.

Mac, one page of Buried Alivecache offcache on

The time moved instead of going away, and the cache image alone is 64 MB: three times all of KOReader's memory. Dropped.

3Now: Blitz paints into our layer, which splits the page

Blitz's painter accepts any drawing target, so ours sits in between. Plain glyph runs go to our glyph cache. Anything else (lines, borders, images, and glyph runs we don't take) is recorded for vello_cpu. vello_cpu runs only if that record draws anything.

Blitz's painter→ our layer→ glyphs: copied from masks + the rest: vello_cpu, only if any→ grey page

Holmes has pages where vello_cpu still has work. On the left is what vello_cpu draws, on the right the finished piece:

92 of Holmes's 387 pages need vello_cpu, which costs about 1 ms more on the Mac. The median page doesn't.

4The masks: each glyph drawn once

The first time a glyph is needed, vello_cpu draws it alone and black on a small square (the same code that drew it before, so the shapes match). The square is cut to the ink and kept. A mask is kept per font, size and quarter-pixel position across. This is every mask this page needed, 237 in all, shown 2× (later pages mostly reuse them):
