# Welcome

ShamsaInk is a reader for e-ink devices, made for Farsi and English books alike.

This short book shows you how to use it. Each chapter is a page or two, and you can try each thing as you read about it.

When you have read it, you can keep it or remove it from the library like any other book.

To begin, tap the right side of this page.

Tap almost anywhere on the page to go forward. Tap the quarter of the page on the left edge to go back.

In a Farsi book this is mirrored: the back quarter is on the right edge, since the book starts on the right.

You can also swipe, as with paper.

If your device has page buttons, the lower one goes forward, whichever way you hold the device. Hold a button for a second to jump to the next or previous chapter.

# The bar and the dock

Tap the top of the page to bring up the controls. Tap the page again to put them away.

**The bar, at the top,** belongs to the device. From the left: back to your library, the chapter you are in, the time, Bluetooth headphones (grey until they are connected: tap them to connect, or to set the narration's volume), the light, the battery and the menu.

![](topbar-book-en.png)

**The dock, at the bottom,** belongs to the book: where you are, and how many pages are left in this part.

![](dock-plain-en.png)

- Tap anywhere on the progress bar to go there, or use the arrows to go to the previous or next chapter.
- After a jump, "Back to page N" takes you back to where you were.
- **Contents** (the list, bottom left) shows the book's chapters. Tap one to go there.
- **Text** (Aa) changes how the book looks.

# Text and page

In the dock, tap **Aa**.

![](text-text-en.png)

On the **Text** tab: text size, line spacing and margins. On the **Page** tab: which way the pages turn.

These settings are kept for each book separately. **Reset** puts this book's back to the defaults.

# Highlights

Hold your finger on a word. Drag to choose more words, then lift and tap **Highlight**.

![](highlight-menu-new-en.png){.small}

To remove a highlight, hold it and tap **Remove highlight**.

Try it on this sentence.

# Light and sleep

In the bar, tap the sun to set the light and, where your device has it, its warmth.

![](light-panel-en.png)

**Dark mode** shows white text on black. **Clean screen** clears the grey shadows that e-ink leaves behind over time. In **Reading settings** (in the menu) you can have the screen cleaned on its own every few pages.

Press the power button to put the device to sleep. If it is left untouched, it sleeps by itself after the time set in **Settings → Sleep after**.

# Your books

ShamsaInk reads EPUB books. Connect the device to a computer and copy them into the **books** folder in ShamsaInk's folder:

- Kobo: `.adds/shamsaink/books/`
- Kindle: `shamsaink/books/`

Folders inside **books** are fine, one level deep.

![](library-guide-en.png)

In the library, **Reading**, **New**, **Finished** and **All** show those books, and the arrows on the right sort them. Hold your finger on a book for its menu: mark it finished, start again, its details, or remove it.

You can find public-domain Farsi books, ready for e-ink, on FarsiEPUB.

# Leaving

In the bar, tap the menu (the three lines), then **Leave**, to go back to your device's own screens. Your place in each book is kept.

![](menu-book-en.png){.small}

How to update or uninstall ShamsaInk is on FarsiEPUB's website.

Happy reading.
