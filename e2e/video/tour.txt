# The tour for the video of the simulated Kobo (e2e/video/README.md): one evening with a book. The opening card, the
# library (an English book, then FarsiEPUB's narrated Buried Alive: record.sh's BOOKS is out/video/books, README.md),
# two acts (a card for each, `caption`) and one more thing (the sleep screen), their steps named beside the screen
# (`title`); the end card lists what ShamsaInk does, more than the tour shows, a point a line. Paced by waits for a
# viewer, not checked against references: not a test (it lives outside e2e/scenarios/).
