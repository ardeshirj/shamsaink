# The tour for the video of the simulated Kobo (e2e/video/README.md): one evening with a book in three acts (a card for
# each, `caption`), its steps named beside the screen (`title`), on FarsiEPUB's narrated Buried Alive; the end card lists
# what ShamsaInk does, more than the tour shows, a point a line. Paced by waits for a viewer, not checked against references: not a test
# (it lives outside e2e/scenarios/).
on: sim
book: books/farsiepub/buried-alive-narrated.epub
env SHAMSAINK_RECORD=/sim/record
forget books/farsiepub/buried-alive-narrated.epub
caption ShamsaInk | A light reader for e-ink, made for Farsi
caption Farsi, done properly | Shaped and set right to left, like a printed page
title A Farsi book
open
resume
wait 2.5
press forward
wait 2.2
press forward
wait 2.4
title Any chapter, two taps
tap page-top
expect menu drawn
wait 1.8
tap dock contents
expect contents: \d+ entries
wait 2
tap contents e2
expect went to document
wait 2.2
caption Yours to adjust | Size, marks and light, each a tap away
title Text size
tap page-top
expect menu drawn
wait 1.6
tap dock text
wait 1.8
tap text size-inc
wait 1.8
tap text size-inc
wait 2
tap text size-dec
wait 1.6
tap text size-dec
wait 1.8
tap 632 1000
wait 1.5
title Highlights
hold 1120 476 to 600 476
expect highlight menu: words chosen
wait 1.8
tap-menu hl-make
expect highlight \d+ made
wait 2
title Dark mode
tap page-top
expect menu drawn
wait 1.4
tap topbar topbar-light
wait 1.8
tap light dark
expect dark mode on
wait 2
tap 632 1000
wait 1.4
tap 632 1000
wait 1.6
press forward
wait 2.4
tap page-top
expect menu drawn
wait 1.4
tap topbar topbar-light
wait 1.6
tap light dark
expect dark mode off
wait 1.6
tap 632 1000
wait 1.4
tap 632 1000
wait 1.6
caption Listen along | The narrated word is underlined as it is spoken, | the voice through Bluetooth headphones
title Read-along
tap page-top                       # behind the card: to the start of «Davood the Hunchback», the chapter of the read-along video
expect menu drawn
tap dock contents
expect contents: \d+ entries
tap contents e3
expect went to document
resume
wait 1.5
hold 167 804                       # a word at the start of a sentence in the first paragraph
expect highlight menu: words chosen
wait 1.6
tap-menu hl-listen                 # Listen from here
expect read aloud from word
wait 22
tap page-top
expect read aloud: stopped
wait 1.2
tap 632 1000
wait 1
title Good night
press power                        # to sleep: a sleep screen (record.sh lays one of FarsiEPUB's in sleep/)
wait 4.5
caption end | Farsi and right to left, done properly | Read-along for narrated books | Light, warmth and a dark mode | Sleep screens: poems or your own pictures | and more… | Learn more and install — farsiepub.org/shamsaink
press power                        # awake again, for close
wait 3
close
