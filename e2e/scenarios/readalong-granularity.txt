# Read-along marks a narrated unit in every line of it, whatever the overlay's granularity (EPUB 3.3: the SMIL may point
# at words, sentences or whole paragraphs). Our own fa-basics narrates its bridge (chapter 3) by a tone with all three
# (e2e/books/fa-basics/README.md): a word at a time (w1..), a sentence at a time (s1..), one paragraph (p3). A cue must
# be marked in one box a line: a word in 1, a sentence over two lines in 2, the paragraph in several. The paragraph's
# single box was the bug of 2026-11-05 (a bar only under its last line: the engine's find gave a block one box). The
# paragraph begins on the chapter's second page: the narration turns the page to it. The reader logs each mark when
# MARK_LOG is set, its page counted from the book's start (the bridge's pages are the book's 5 and 6, at the fixture's
# settings, on the Kobo's screen). No headphones needed; the beeps can be heard on the Kobo. About 45 s.
#     make e2e SCENARIO=e2e/scenarios/readalong-granularity.txt
on: sim kobo                       # the Kindle has no sound of ours yet
fixture: fa-basics                 # the book opens at the bridge's first page
book fa-basics
env MARK_LOG=1
open
tap page-top
expect menu drawn
tap dock read-aloud
expect read aloud from word 0
expect mark w1: document 3 page 5, 1 boxes
expect-in 30 mark s2: document 3 page 5, 2 boxes      # the second sentence, over two lines
expect-in 30 mark p3: document 3 page 6, ([3-9]|\d\d) boxes  # the third paragraph, on the next page
tap page-top
expect read aloud: stopped
close
