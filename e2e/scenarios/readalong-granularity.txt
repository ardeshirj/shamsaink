# Read-along marks a narrated unit in every line of it, whatever the overlay's granularity (EPUB 3.3: the SMIL may point
# at words, sentences or whole paragraphs). IDPF's Moby-Dick sample (CC-BY-SA 3.0; the download is checked against its
# SHA-256, never committed) has all three in its first chapter: words (c01w00001..3), a sentence (c01s0002) and
# paragraphs (c01p0002..). A cue must be marked in one box a line: a word in 1, a sentence over several lines in several,
# a paragraph in several. The paragraph's single box was the bug of 2026-11-05 (a bar only under its last line: the
# engine's find gave a block one box). The reader logs each mark when MARK_LOG is set. No headphones needed. The first
# paragraph is read about 76 s after the sentence before it: the scenario takes about two minutes.
#     make e2e SCENARIO=e2e/scenarios/readalong-granularity.txt
on: sim kobo
fetch https://github.com/IDPF/epub3-samples/releases/download/20230704/moby-dick-mo.epub books/idpf/moby-dick-mo.epub 749a9de094c26bc79351722a2bf320845dc5f6af2d5bf41a96d720d723b29a4c
forget books/idpf/moby-dick-mo.epub
env MARK_LOG=1
open books/idpf/moby-dick-mo.epub
tap page-top
expect menu drawn
tap dock read-aloud
expect read aloud from word 0
expect mark c01w00001: document \d+ page \d+, 1 boxes
expect mark c01s0002: document \d+ page \d+, ([2-9]|\d\d) boxes
expect-in 100 mark c01p0002: document \d+ page \d+, ([2-9]|\d\d) boxes
tap page-top
expect read aloud: stopped
close
