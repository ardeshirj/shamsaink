# The watchdog leaves a healthy reader alone, on a faster clock (see watchdog-freeze.txt): a book idle, then a narration
# playing (to a sink with no headphones: the loop and the clock, not the sound), then the book left the normal way. Not
# covered: sleep and wake (sleep.txt, sleep-more.txt, sleep-narration.txt), the library list left alone.

book fa-basics

tap dock read-aloud
