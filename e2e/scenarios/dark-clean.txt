# Dark mode chosen in a book, the reader left and the book opened again: the page turns are counted against dark mode's
# "Clean every" (the fixture's clean_dark 5), not light mode's (clean_light 3). In 0.5.0-beta only the start's own
# settings knew the screen was dark, so a reopened book cleaned every 3 pages in dark mode.
on: sim kobo                       # the taps that close the panel are at the Kobo's pixels
book fa-basics
open books/test/fa-basics.epub
tap page-top
expect menu drawn
tap topbar topbar-light
tap light dark
expect dark mode on
tap 632 1000                       # the panel closed, then the bar and the dock
tap 632 1000
close
open books/test/fa-basics.epub
turn forward                       # forward and back: the book opens near its end (the fixture's place)
expect page \d+: drawn( before)?, \d+ ms
turn back
expect page \d+: drawn( before)?, \d+ ms
turn forward
expect page \d+: drawn( before)?, \d+ ms  # the third: light mode's count would clean here
turn back
expect page \d+: drawn( before)?, \d+ ms
turn forward
expect page \d+: drawn( before)?, cleaned            # the fifth: dark mode's
