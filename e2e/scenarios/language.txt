# "Follow book language" (the library's Settings): with it on, a Farsi book's panels are in Farsi; with it off, in English.
# Our own Farsi book (e2e/books/fa-basics, its fixture the last book read), its dock snapped both ways. The switch's own
# effect, on and off, is in the design spec (library-settings, lang-follow: the walk presses it and its undo); this is the
# journey on a book. The fixture's settings.json has no english_only: the switch is on at the start, and the reader's
# files are put back after.
# The library's controls are tapped at their kept places: devtest finds a screen's controls in the render of the
# interface's language, which it takes to be the last book's (fa), while the library is always in English; to mend in
# devtest (script.go, scenario.go). On the simulator only snapped: on the Kobo the bar's clock and battery change.
on: sim kobo
fixture: fa-basics
refs: images fa                     # our own book: its pages committed as pictures (e2e/refs/)
book fa-basics
open
tap page-top                       # the bar and the dock, in Farsi
expect menu drawn in .*, in fa\)
snap dock-farsi
tap topbar topbar-start            # back to the library
expect library: .* shown in
tap 1180 84                        # the library's ≡ (library-reading-en's box)
tap 1076 207                       # its Settings (menu-library-en's box)
tap 1169 354                       # Follow book language: off, English only (library-settings-follow-en's box)
expect interface language: English only
tap 1169 354                       # on again: the undo the walk presses
expect interface language: follows the book
tap 1169 354                       # off again, for the book
expect interface language: English only
tap 632 1500                       # off the panel, under the rows: it closes
tap 632 446                        # the same book, the first row (library-reading-en's e0)
expect document \d+ page \d+ shown in
tap page-top                       # the bar and the dock, in English
expect menu drawn in .*, in en\)
snap dock-english
tap 84 84                          # the English bar's back arrow (topbar-book-en's box): back to the library
expect library: .* shown in
tap 1180 84                        # the library's ≡
tap 1076 298                       # Leave (menu-library-en's box): the reader left, as close does
expect library: left
