# More sleeps on the Kobo than sleep.txt's one, with the same switches (docs/devices/kobo/README.md, "Sleep"), each
# woken by the clock's alarm (WAKE_AFTER): from the library, twice in a row in a book, and the reader left at once after
# a wake, before Wi-Fi is back (left 7 s after waking on 2026-11-12, Wi-Fi was not back in Nickel). Then the Kobo must
# still be on its network with its chip without errors (expect-online). During a narration: sleep-narration.txt.
# Each expect after a press power waits out the sleep: Wi-Fi is off from just before the "asleep" line until the wake.
# The Kobo must be unplugged, with Wi-Fi on.
#     make e2e SCENARIO=e2e/scenarios/sleep-more.txt
on: kobo                           # the simulator does not suspend
book fa-basics                     # the last book read (the default fixture opens it at its bridge)
env WAKE_AFTER=15
open
# from the library
tap page-top                       # the bar and the dock
expect menu drawn
tap topbar topbar-start            # back to the library
expect library: .* shown in
press power
expect-in 90 asleep: sleep screen shown
expect woken by the test's alarm
expect-in 15 awake: page back
expect-in 30 Wi-Fi back in
tap 632 446                        # the book again, the first row (library-reading-en's e0)
expect document \d+ page \d+ shown in
# twice in a row, in the book
press power
expect-in 90 asleep: sleep screen shown
expect woken by the test's alarm
expect-in 30 Wi-Fi back in
press power
expect-in 90 asleep: sleep screen shown
expect woken by the test's alarm
expect-in 30 Wi-Fi back in
# left at once after a wake, Wi-Fi not back yet
press power
expect-in 90 asleep: sleep screen shown
expect woken by the test's alarm
expect-in 15 awake: page back
close
expect-online 60
