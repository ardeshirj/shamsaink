# A sleep during a narration on the Kobo: the narration stopped first, then Bluetooth and Wi-Fi switched off through the
# chip's own switches with Kobo's sound service just in use (docs/devices/kobo/README.md, "Sleep"; on 2026-11-12 the
# sound and speech services timing out on the chip's link, Bluetooth switched off under them, crashed its firmware).
# Our own fa-basics, opened at its narrated bridge (e2e/fixtures/fa-basics); its tone to the sink with or without
# headphones. Woken by the clock's alarm (WAKE_AFTER), then read aloud once more, the book left,
# and the Kobo on its network with its chip without errors. The Kobo unplugged, with Wi-Fi on.
#     make e2e SCENARIO=e2e/scenarios/sleep-narration.txt
on: kobo                           # the simulator does not suspend
fixture: fa-basics
book fa-basics
env WAKE_AFTER=15
open
tap page-top
expect menu drawn
tap dock read-aloud
expect read aloud from word
wait 5
press power                        # the narration stopped, then to sleep
expect-in 90 read aloud: stopped
expect-in 90 asleep: sleep screen shown
expect woken by the test's alarm
expect-in 15 awake: page back
expect-in 30 Wi-Fi back in
tap page-top                       # read aloud again after the wake: the player opened anew
expect menu drawn
tap dock read-aloud
expect read aloud from word
wait 5
tap page-top
expect read aloud: stopped
close
expect-online 60
