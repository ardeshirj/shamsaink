# A catalogue (docs/DESIGN.md, "Books from catalogues") on the simulated Kobo (CI, every push: the group reading), and
# by hand on the Kobo and the Kindle after a change to reader/opds, catalogues.go or the library's loop: `make e2e
# SCENARIO=e2e/scenarios/catalogue.txt` (on the Kindle DEVICE=kindle). No site is reached: devtest serves a catalogue from this computer (catalogue-serve): its main page two
# sections, "Test books" holding two of our test books and "By language" (a link of no kind, a section of sections);
# every page of books has two facet groups, Language and Narrated (which no book is); it is added to the device's
# catalogues.json as the last catalogue shown; Wi-Fi is on (SSH; the simulator's switched on), so nothing is asked.
# Browse and filter (the funnel): Test books, then its Farsi facet, Show 1 book, the book downloaded (its row then
# Read); a facet that matches nothing, closed, Clear filters; By language, its Farsi, back up; then the catalogue
# chosen again (back to its main page, no read), its Test books row, and the book opened from it. The catalogue's files
# and the books downloaded are removed after; the reader's own put back.
on: sim kobo kindle
catalogue-serve en-basics fa-basics
library
tap library shelf-catalogues
expect-in 30 library: catalogues: E2E Catalogue
expect-in 30 catalogue: http://.*/catalog.xml read in \d+ ms \(2 sections, 0 books\)
tap catalogue:sections catalogue-filter
expect library: catalogue filters \(2 choices\)
tap catalogue-filters:test-front filter-0
expect library: catalogue section "Test books"
expect-in 30 catalogue: http://.*/books.xml read in \d+ ms \(0 sections, 2 books\)
expect library: catalogue filters \(7 choices\)
tap catalogue-filters:test-books filter-4
expect library: catalogue facet http://.*/books-fa.xml
expect-in 30 catalogue: http://.*/books-fa.xml read in \d+ ms \(0 sections, 1 books\)
expect library: catalogue filters \(7 choices\)
tap catalogue-filters:test-books filters-show
expect library: catalogue filters closed \(1 rows\)
tap catalogue e0
expect library: urn:e2e:fa-basics asked for
expect-in 60 catalogue: urn:e2e:fa-basics downloaded in .* s: books/e2e/fa-basics.epub
tap catalogue:sections catalogue-filter
expect library: catalogue filters \(7 choices\)
tap catalogue-filters:test-books filter-6
expect library: catalogue facet http://.*/none.xml
expect-in 30 catalogue: http://.*/none.xml read in \d+ ms \(0 sections, 0 books\)
expect library: catalogue filters \(7 choices\)
tap catalogue-filters:test-books filters-close
expect library: catalogue filters closed \(0 rows\)
tap catalogue:no-match catalogue-clear-all
expect library: catalogue filters cleared
expect-in 30 catalogue: http://.*/catalog.xml read in \d+ ms \(2 sections, 0 books\)
tap catalogue:sections catalogue-filter
tap catalogue-filters:test-front filter-1
expect library: catalogue section "By language"
expect-in 30 catalogue: http://.*/langs.xml read in \d+ ms \(2 sections, 0 books\)
expect library: catalogue filters \(2 choices\)
tap catalogue-filters:test-language filter-1
expect library: catalogue section "Farsi"
expect-in 30 catalogue: http://.*/books-fa.xml read in \d+ ms \(0 sections, 1 books\)
expect library: catalogue filters \(7 choices\)
tap catalogue-filters:test-language filters-back
expect library: catalogue filters, back to "By language"
tap catalogue-filters:test-language filters-close
expect library: catalogue filters closed \(2 rows\)
tap catalogue catalogue-choose
tap catalogue-menu catalogue-1
expect library: catalogue E2E Catalogue, its main page
tap catalogue e0
expect library: catalogue section "Test books"
expect-in 30 catalogue: http://.*/books.xml read in \d+ ms \(0 sections, 2 books\)
tap catalogue e1
expect-in 30 library: books/e2e/fa-basics.epub read from the catalogue
expect-in 30 fa-basics.epub: document \d+ page \d+ shown in .*right to left true
