# The watchdog catches a reader that answers nothing (docs/design.md, "Keeping the reader alive"), on a faster clock: the
# reader beats every second and the watcher looks every 4 s, so a freeze is caught in 8 to 12 s, not 20 to 30. Run with
#     make e2e SCENARIO=e2e/kobo-watchdog-freeze.txt
# Bluetooth must not have run in this boot (the launcher would reboot the Kobo instead of starting Nickel again). Needs
# SSH while the reader is open; if that drops, expect-nickel keeps asking.
env HEARTBEAT_EVERY=1s WATCH_EVERY=4
open
wait 6
freeze
wait 6
expect watchdog: the reader \(\d+\) has not answered
expect reader exited
expect-nickel 60
