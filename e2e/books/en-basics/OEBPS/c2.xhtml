The Market

The market is already full of people. The sellers call out loudly: tomatoes, apples, grapes and fresh bread. An old woman chooses oranges one by one and smells each of them. A small boy holds his father's hand and looks at the sweets with wide eyes.

I buy a bag of figs and talk with the seller for a while. He says he has worked in the market for thirty years, and that his father worked in the same place before him. He knows all his customers by name, and he knows who likes sweet fruit and who likes sour fruit.

Next to the market there is a small café. I sit outside, drink cold water and watch the people passing by. A bus stops at the corner, some people get off and others get on, and the bus goes on its way.
