Morning

It is seven in the morning. The sun is rising over the roofs, and the street is still quiet. I open the window and hear the sparrows in the tree beside the house. My neighbour walks out with his dog, and we both say good morning. I make coffee, eat bread and cheese, and sit at the kitchen table. I have no work today, and I want to walk all over the city.

After breakfast I take a small notebook and a pen. I like to write down what I see: people, shops, trees and old houses. When I write, I remember everything better. I close the door and go down the stairs to the street.
