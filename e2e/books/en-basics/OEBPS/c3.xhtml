The Park

In the afternoon I go to the city park. The trees are tall, and there are old wooden benches under them. Children play football on the grass, and two old men play chess in the shade.

I sit on a bench near the fountain and open my notebook. I write about the sound of the water, the smell of the flowers and the birds that drink at the edge of the fountain. Time passes quickly, and I do not notice that the sun is going down.

In the evening the lamps come on along the path. I walk home slowly; the air is cool and pleasant. At home I make tea and read what I wrote during the day, then I turn off the light and go to sleep.
