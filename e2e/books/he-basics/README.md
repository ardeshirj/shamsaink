# he-basics

A day in the city, in Hebrew: a title page and three short chapters (morning, the market, the library), and a
contents. Stated as Hebrew (`dc:language he`), pages turning right to left (`page-progression-direction="rtl"`, `dir` in
the markup and the CSS). The library's second paragraph is pointed (niqqud), for Frank Ruhl Libre's vowel points.

Tests (`e2e/scenarios/languages/rtl-he.txt`): Hebrew pages in Frank Ruhl Libre (the engine puts the book's script's
font first), the interface in English mirrored (`en-rtl`), contents, pages turned right to left, a highlight made and
removed, the book left by the mirrored bar's back arrow.

The text is ours, written for these tests (Apache-2.0, as the repository). **Not yet checked by a reader of Hebrew.**
