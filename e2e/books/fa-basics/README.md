# fa-basics

A day in Isfahan, in Persian: a title page and four short chapters (morning, the square, the bridge, night), each about
two pages at the default fixture's text size, and a contents. Stated as Farsi (`dc:language fa`), pages turning right to
left (`page-progression-direction="rtl"`), the direction also in its CSS, as rtlbook's books. No pictures, no narration.

Tests (`e2e/scenarios/rtl-fa.txt`): Farsi pages in the reader's own fonts, the bar and dock in Farsi, contents, pages
turned right to left, a highlight made and removed.

The text is ours, written for these tests (Apache-2.0, as the repository).
