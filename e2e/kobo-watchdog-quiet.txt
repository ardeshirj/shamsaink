# The watchdog leaves a healthy reader alone, on a faster clock (see kobo-watchdog-freeze.txt): a book idle, then a narration
# playing (to a sink with no headphones: the loop and the clock, not the sound), then the book left the normal way. Not
# covered: sleep and wake (the Kobo suspends and takes SSH with it; by hand), the library list left alone.
#     make e2e SCENARIO=e2e/kobo-watchdog-quiet.txt
